What it is

@exploring.temple is a newer MMC-owned page dedicated to culture, history and spirituality — संस्कृती (culture), इतिहास (history) and अध्यात्म (spirituality). The page seeks out hidden and lesser-known temples across India, researching and documenting their history before turning it into content.

It's early — 17 posts in — with an ongoing Navratri series as its first running content format.

Why it matters to MMC

It's a second proof point that MMC can build an audience from zero on a niche, culturally-rooted idea — the same research-to-content discipline we bring to client work, tested first on our own page.
